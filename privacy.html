Tinsimu ta Vakriste

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Tinsimu ta Vakriste is an offline Xitsonga hymnbook. For privacy questions, contact nghonisiyabonga@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The hymns and liturgical readings are included in the app. Favourites, the reading text size, and the theme are stored on your device. We do not require an account or upload your hymn searches, favourites, or reading history to our own server. Removing the app normally removes its local preferences, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Advertising and data sent to Google

When internet access is available and advertising is permitted, the app may show a Google AdMob banner while you browse. The app requests non-personalized ads, but that does not mean the advertising SDK collects no data. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which may indicate an approximate location), interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic data, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The SDK sends this data with transport encryption. Google's processing and retention are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent before requesting ads. Hymns still work without an internet connection or an ad.

Support and external services

The Support screen displays voluntary bank transfer details. The app does not receive your banking credentials or process a transfer. If you choose to make a transfer in your bank's app, your bank handles that transaction under its own privacy terms. There is no live Paystack payment link in this version. If one is added, this policy will be updated before release.

Sharing a hymn uses your device's sharing options. The destination you choose handles information you send according to its own privacy terms. We do not receive the content of your shares.

Retention, requests, and changes

We do not run an app account or a server that stores your hymns or preferences. Local preferences remain until you remove them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, subject to device backups. For information that Google processes for ads, see Google's privacy controls and policy. Contact nghonisiyabonga@gmail.com with privacy questions. We will update this page when app data practices change and show the new effective date.
